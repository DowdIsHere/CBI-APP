Option 1: TEMPORARY LINK (Keep computer running)
-------------------------------------------------

1. Double-click this file:

   create-test-link.bat

2. Wait for QR code to appear

3. Take screenshot of QR code

4. Send to testers with these instructions:
   "Install Expo Go app, scan this QR code"

5. Keep your computer running while they test


================================================================================

Option 2: PERMANENT LINK (Hosted by Expo)
------------------------------------------

1. Double-click this file:

   publish-for-testing.bat

2. Wait for link (looks like: exp://u.expo.dev/...)

3. Copy the link

4. Send to testers with these instructions:
   "Install Expo Go app, open this link"

5. Link works forever - you can close your computer
